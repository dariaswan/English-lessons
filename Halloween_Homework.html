3 · Judy's blog

Reading

A · Read Judy's blog.

Judy's blog · The best Halloween ever? 🎃

Last Saturday Andy, Greg and I went trick-or-treating. I was a witch again. My costume was older than Andy's, but I love it!

We visited twelve houses. The first house was the scariest. A man opened the door in a skeleton costume and Greg screamed! It was the funniest moment of the night. At the second house, an old lady gave us apples. Apples! Greg wasn't happy.

The best house was number 7. They gave us big bars of chocolate, bigger than my hand! We walked for two hours. It was colder than last year, and my feet were tired.

At home I counted my sweets. I had 35, Andy had 30, and Greg had only 20, because he ate a lot on the way! Andy is more patient than Greg.

Next year I want a better costume. Maybe a vampire, like Daniel? 🧛
