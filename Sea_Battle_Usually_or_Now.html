  1. Choose a square. Say the sentence.
    “Mia usually goes to yoga.” “The twins are sleeping.”
  2. Your partner answers: “Hit!”, “Miss!” or “You sank my ship!”
  3. A sentence with a mistake doesn’t count. Your teacher decides! Watch out for am / is / are, -s and -ing.
  4. ⭐ Extra: when it’s a miss, say the sentence with not: “Mia doesn’t usually go to yoga.”
  5. The first player to sink all the ships wins.
